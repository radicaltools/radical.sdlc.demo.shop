## Steps

1. place an order
2. send POST /orders/{id}/pay and POST /orders/{id}/cancel at the same time
3. probe the Warehouse for reservations made in between

## What differs

```text
Expected: a refused payment reserves nothing, or the reservation is released
Actual: pay 409 'A cancelled order cannot become paid' while cancel 200; the Warehouse made one reservation for the cancelled order (gap of one reservation id between two probes). Shown in 10 of 10 attempts in one run and 9 of 10 in the other (19 of 20 across the two runs); in the other attempts the payment won and the cancel was refused
Seen: 2 of 2 runs (19 of 20 attempts)
Where it probably comes from: services/orders/src/server.ts, POST /orders/:id/pay: book.move re-checks the lifecycle only after await neighbours.reserve, and the reservation is not released when the move is refused (guess; orderBook.ts move() throws WrongMove)
A test that would catch it: a payment whose order is cancelled while the reserve is in flight is answered 409 and leaves no reservation, or releases it; test with a Warehouse stand-in that delays the reserve
```
