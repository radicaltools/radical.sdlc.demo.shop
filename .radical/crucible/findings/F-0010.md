## Steps

1. POST http://localhost:4703/events {id:'f1-…:order.paid', type:'order.paid', orderId: 767256} (orderId a number)
2. GET http://localhost:4703/notifications?orderId=767256

## What differs

```text
Expected: a 4xx for orderId not a string, nothing recorded; or the notification is listed for its order
Actual: 200 {eventId, orderId:767256, text:'Your payment arrived.'} is recorded, and the list for 767256 is []
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: services/notifications/src/notifier.ts of() compares n.orderId === orderId: the query string '767256' never equals the number 767256 (guess: no body validation in services/notifications/src/server.ts line 9 for POST /events)
A test that would catch it: services/notifications: POST /events with a numeric orderId is refused with a 4xx and recorded nowhere
```
